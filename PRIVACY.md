# TDTbot Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 23, 2026**

TDTbot is a Discord community-management and moderation bot operated for The Dream Team Discord community.

## Data TDTbot accesses

TDTbot may access information provided through Discord that is necessary to operate its features, including:

- Discord user and member IDs;
- server membership and role information;
- member join dates;
- messages and message content in channels where TDTbot operates;
- direct messages sent to TDTbot;
- message and reaction metadata; and
- Discord channel and message identifiers.

## How the data is used

This information is used to provide TDTbot's community-management functionality, including:

- member onboarding;
- role assignment and management;
- Code of Conduct acknowledgement;
- moderation and spam detection;
- traditional text commands;
- inactivity management;
- administrative utilities; and
- communication between users and the server moderation team.

TDTbot does not use Discord data to train machine-learning or artificial-intelligence models.

## Data stored outside Discord

TDTbot stores a limited amount of operational data on the system where the bot runs.

This may include Discord user IDs, member activity timestamps, supporter enrollment information, administrative configuration, and Discord message or channel identifiers required by specific bot features.

TDTbot does not intentionally maintain an off-platform archive of ordinary Discord message contents.

Messages reported by moderation features or direct messages sent to TDTbot may be copied to private moderation channels within Discord so that authorized moderators can review and respond to them.

## Data sharing

TDTbot does not sell user data.

Information processed by TDTbot is used for operation and administration of the Discord community and may be visible to authorized server moderators when necessary for moderation or support.

## Data retention

Operational metadata is retained for as long as it is needed by the corresponding TDTbot feature or until it is removed by an administrator.

## Contact

Questions or requests concerning TDTbot's handling of Discord data may be directed to the administrators of The Dream Team Discord community, whom can be reached by direct messaging TDTbot.
